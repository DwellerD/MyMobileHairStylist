# Account Deletion

Last updated: 2026-06-08

## In-app deletion

When enabled in production, users can request account deletion from the profile/settings area.

## Request by email

If in-app deletion is unavailable, contact:

- delete@your-domain.com

Include the account email and a brief request. Verification may be required.

## What gets deleted

- Authentication account
- Profile data not required for legal or financial record retention

## Retention exceptions

Some records may be retained as required by law, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or accounting obligations.
